# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [insert date]*

Wyte Tech Company ("we", "us") operates LabGuru. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Account info:** when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo via Firebase Authentication.

**Usage data:** analyses you've read, Q&A answers, and study progress, so your progress can sync across your devices.

**Payment info:** subscription payments are processed by Flutterwave. We receive confirmation of successful payment and a reference ID — we never see or store your card details.

**Notification token:** if you enable notifications, we store a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you study reminders and updates.

## How We Use Information

To provide and sync the app's features, verify and manage your Premium subscription, send notifications you've opted into, and improve the app.

## Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, messaging) and Flutterwave (payment processing). These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention & Your Rights

You can clear your read history and Q&A answers anytime from My Progress (a Premium feature). To request deletion of your account data entirely, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

LabGuru is intended for MLS students and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
